# Learning Reference Policy — Learn AI Workflow

## Purpose

Keep learning materials credible, relevant, and traceable without requiring academic journals for every lesson.

## Source Priority

1. **Official documentation — primary source.** Use it for library APIs, syntax, parameters, expected behavior, installation, and recommended usage.
2. **High-quality tutorials — supporting source.** Use them when they explain concepts or workflows more clearly than reference documentation. Prefer sources with clear authorship, examples, and maintenance.
3. **Academic papers and journals — when relevant.** Use them when a lesson needs research evidence or deeper grounding, especially for statistics, machine learning, and specific AI methods. They are not mandatory for every lesson.

## Accuracy and Attribution Rules

- Sources must support the particular concept being taught.
- Distinguish **sources actually consulted for a lesson** from **sources recommended for further reading**.
- Do not claim that a source was used historically unless the lesson record confirms it.
- Do not invent citations, papers, authors, or bibliographies.
- When sources disagree, explain the discrepancy and prioritize the most authoritative and relevant source for the specific claim.
- Keep explanations appropriate to the learner's current roadmap stage.
