# Project Constitution — Learn A.I Workflow for Data Science

## Purpose
This document defines the operating rules of the learning system. The system is designed to support the goal of becoming a **Data Scientist / Data Analyst who is AI-native**, with AI acting as a **force multiplier**, not a replacement for data foundations.

## Authority Hierarchy
When information conflicts, use this order:

1. **Project Standards / Constitution**
2. **Official Roadmap (Single Source of Truth)**
3. **Validated Learning State**
4. **Validated Evidence / Progress Log**
5. **Historical Records**

Historical records are references. They do not automatically override active standards or current validated state.

## Core Rules
- The roadmap may not be changed without explicit user approval.
- Progress may only be changed when supported by learning evidence and a validation decision.
- Do not infer completion merely because a topic was mentioned or discussed.
- Before presenting progress, compare the proposed progress with the latest validated state and recent evidence.
- If there is a conflict or uncertainty, review the available sources before changing state.
- Approved formats, terminology, order, and workflow are constraints, not suggestions.
- Do not improvise a new dashboard or tracking format when a standard already exists.
- Dashboard is a presentation layer; validated progress state is the source of truth.
- AI may assist thinking, debugging, and feedback, but the learner remains responsible for understanding and validation.

## Learning Philosophy
The system values:
- understanding over checklist completion,
- evidence over memory,
- transfer over rote repetition,
- retention over one-time performance,
- practical problem solving over passive consumption.

## Progress Status Vocabulary
Use these meanings consistently:
- **Learned**: material has been studied.
- **Validated**: performance has passed the current evaluation.
- **Retained**: understanding remains demonstrated after a meaningful interval/retrieval review.
- **Mastered**: ability is consistently demonstrated in new contexts.

A high score or successful challenge does not automatically prove permanent mastery.

## Separation of Concerns
- **Roadmap** answers: What should be learned and in what order?
- **Current Progress State** answers: Where is the learner now?
- **Evidence Log** answers: Why is that progress state justified?
- **Learning Protocol** answers: How should learning and validation happen?
- **Dashboard Specification** answers: How should progress be displayed?
